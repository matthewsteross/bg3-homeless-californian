## 2. The two Antigravity suggestions
**(a) "Base" race preset with all-zero SubRaceUUID - the premise is half right, the risk is low.**
- Vanilla `CharacterCreationPresets.lsx` has 58 presets. Zero SubRaceUUID is used only by races that have NO subraces (Human `0eb594cb`, Githyanki `bdf9b779`).
- Vanilla never emits a zero-SubRace preset for a race that has subraces (checked: 0 cases).
- HC emits 4 such "Base" presets (`generate_mod.py:1180`) for a race that does have 3 subraces. That is off-pattern, but nothing in vanilla suggests it breaks anything; it is most likely a dead entry never selected. It is also part of the build you already confirmed works in-game.
- Recommendation: **optional cleanup** - drop the `("Base", zero)` row so HC matches vanilla's shape exactly. Low value, tiny risk, not play-tested either way. Your call.

**(b) `SelectPassives(..., CaliforniaIdentity)` without a `DefaultValues\Passives.lsx` entry - NOT needed. No change.**
- Vanilla uses 11 distinct SelectPassives selector ids in Progressions; only 3 appear in vanilla `DefaultValues\Passives.lsx` by SelectorId (DraconicAncestry, FavoredEnemy, NaturalExplorer).
- 8 selectors (FightingStyle, Metamagic, Maneuvers, PactBoon, WarlockInvocations, HuntersPrey, DruidCircleLand, TotemWarriorPassive) work with no SelectorId default. (FightingStyle also has table-keyed defaults, but Metamagic has none at all.)
- HC's progression has the `SelectorId` attribute set (`generate_mod.py:893`). Same conclusion as the earlier "missing SelectorId" claim: already handled.

## 5. Codex mechanics audit (run through Solo; every finding re-checked by me)
Codex's own shell helper still fails ("setup refresh had errors"), so each read had to be approved in Solo; I approved only read-only `rg`/`Get-Content` commands and two in-memory Python scans (read before approving). It was not allowed to change anything.

Reported 2 findings, plus 1 it withdrew:
1. **Proposition 47 (`Passive.txt:23`, `generate_mod.py:1911`) - REAL, minor.** Description says "whenever you hit an enemy with a melee attack" but the condition is only `IsMeleeAttack()`, so hitting an ally/neutral also grants the vest. Vanilla `Divination_Damage_Fire` (`SharedDev/.../Passive.txt:613`) uses `... and Enemy(context.Target)` for the same kind of OnDamage trigger. Fix: `IsMeleeAttack() and Enemy(context.Target)`. Low risk; waits for your OK.
2. **Encampment Sweep (`Passive.txt:67`) "buffs the attacker" - NOT CONFIRMED, probably fine. No change recommended.** Vanilla `MAG_ChargedLightning_ChargeOnTakeDamage_Cloak_Passive` (Gustav `Passive.txt`) is an OnDamaged passive that charges its wearer with `ApplyStatus(SELF,...)`, which is the same shape HC uses. In OnDamaged, SELF and the un-prefixed form both resolve to the passive's owner in vanilla examples, so Codex's "remove SELF" would be a no-op. (Not play-tested; if Encampment Sweep ever misfires in-game, this is the first place to look.)
3. **Feral Screech donor `Shout_MAG_Thunder_ThunderClap` "missing" - FALSE ALARM.** It lives in GustavDev (`GustavDev/Stats/Generated/Data/Spell_Shout.txt:687`), which was not in the old extraction. I extracted Gustav/GustavDev/Honour stats into the gitignored `temp_race\Public\` so future checks include them. With all five vanilla packs, Codex and I both found every donor and every SpellList/PassiveList entry resolves.

Lesson: three "findings" across today's AI reviews were wrong because of incomplete vanilla data or misread context rules. Only #1 survived.
